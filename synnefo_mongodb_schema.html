MongoDB Database Schema — Normalized Design

A student-centric operational database for admissions, programs, multi-program batches, sessions, attendance, assignments, assessments, internship, placement, finance, communication, documents and audit.

MongoDB / Mongoose-ready Atomic fields 3NF principles Historical records preserved No reporting duplication

1. Design Decisions

Student is the long-lived entity.

Current batch/program context must never own historical attendance, assessments, submissions or payments.

Batch is operational.

A batch may contain students from multiple programs. The many-to-many batch↔program relationship is therefore explicit.

Assignments and projects are one entity.

The same assignments domain supports academic and internship work through context references.

No derived storage.

Do not store attendance percentage, outstanding balance, completion percentage or similar values as source-of-truth fields. Calculate them from operational records.

Separate sensitive domains.

Grievances and disciplinary records are independent collections with restricted access.

Authentication is separate.

users represents login identity; students and staff hold domain profiles.

What “3NF” means here: fields are atomic and facts are stored once at their natural ownership level. We do not create a collection simply because two collections can be joined; we create one when the relationship has its own identity, attributes, history or lifecycle.

5. Core Index Strategy

Student

student_code unique; normalized email/phone indexes as required.

Enrollment

{student_id, status}, {student_id, start_date}, and a uniqueness rule for one active enrollment if enforced by application/partial index.

Batch

batch_code unique; {status, start_date}.

Attendance

Unique {session_id, student_id}; {student_id, session_id}; session-date queries through session indexes.

Assignments

{batch_id, deadline} where relevant; unique {assignment_id, student_id} for target/submission relationships.

Finance

Unique transaction/reference IDs where provider guarantees uniqueness; indexes on student and payment status/date.

Placement

Indexes on company, opening, drive, student and application status/stage.

Audit

{entity_type, entity_id, created_at} and {actor_user_id, created_at}.

6. Important Integrity Rules

  • Never rewrite old attendance because of a batch change.
  • Never move an old attendance record to the student's new batch.
  • A cross-batch attendance record references the actual session only; no reason field is required.
  • A batch may be connected to multiple programs.
  • A curriculum item is program-owned; same display name does not make two items identical.
  • Do not store attendance percentage as authoritative data.
  • Do not store outstanding balance as authoritative data; derive it from fee obligations and realized payments.
  • Do not duplicate student name/program/batch in every operational document unless it is explicitly a historical snapshot requirement.
  • Do not embed disciplinary/grievance data inside students.
  • Do not embed unbounded attendance, submissions or payments arrays inside students.
  • Use application/service-layer transactions for multi-document operations such as batch transfer and payment recording.
MongoDB transaction note: Because important workflows touch several collections, use MongoDB transactions for operations that must succeed or fail together—for example creating a batch enrollment and closing the previous one.

7. Student Lifecycle Through the Database

1. Admission

users → students → program_enrollments → fee_accounts

2. Batch allocation

batch_programs identifies programs served by a batch; batch_enrollments connects the student to the operational batch.

3. Training

sessions → attendance_records; assignments → assignment_targets → assignment_submissions; exams → exam_attempts.

4. Internship

internships contains the internship engagement; internship work is represented by assignments using internship context.

5. Placement

companies → job_openings → placement_drives → placement_applications → tests/interviews/offers/joining.

6. Exit

certificates, student status/history, final finance state and placement/internship records remain independently queryable.
